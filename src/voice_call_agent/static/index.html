What is an AI telecaller?+

It is a voice assistant that answers incoming calls or makes approved outbound calls. It can answer FAQs, capture lead details, qualify prospects, book appointments, and send a summary to your team.

Which languages are supported?+

SK Voice Agents can be configured for Telugu, Hindi, and English. We can discuss additional language needs for your workflow.

Can it replace my receptionist or sales team?+

It works best as a helpful first line of response: handling routine questions, basic qualification, bookings, and follow-ups so your people can focus on complex conversations and closing.

Is AI telecalling legal in India?+

Calling needs to follow applicable TRAI, DLT, DND, consent, and data-use requirements. We support compliance-aware workflows, while your business remains responsible for lawful use of customer data and campaigns.

Can it integrate with our CRM or calendar?+

Yes. Lead details and call outcomes can be sent to CRM tools, Google Sheets, calendars, WhatsApp, and email, subject to the integration scope.

What if the AI does not understand a caller?+

The agent can ask the caller to repeat, offer a language option, arrange a callback, or escalate to a team member based on your setup.
